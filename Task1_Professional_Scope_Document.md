# Task 1 – Professional Scope Document

## Project Scope Document

### Project Title
Customer Churn Analysis Project

### Project Objective
Investigate the factors contributing to customer churn, identify key trends and root causes, and provide actionable recommendations to help reduce customer attrition and improve customer retention.

### Expected Deliverable
A professional report summarizing the analysis of customer churn, key findings, root causes, recommendations, and proposed next steps for the management team.

### Intended Audience
- Project Manager
- Senior Management
- Customer Success Team
- Business Strategy Team

### Expected Completion Timeline
Project duration: **2 weeks**

- Week 1: Gather and analyze customer data.
- Week 2: Prepare findings, recommendations, and final report.

### Scope (Included)
- Review available customer churn data.
- Identify patterns and trends contributing to customer churn.
- Analyze possible causes of customer dissatisfaction.
- Develop practical recommendations to improve customer retention.
- Present findings in a professional report.

### Out of Scope
- Implementing recommended business changes.
- Developing new software or customer management systems.
- Conducting customer surveys outside the existing project scope.

### Key Milestones
1. Project kickoff
2. Data collection
3. Data analysis
4. Root cause identification
5. Recommendation development
6. Final report submission

### Assumptions
- Required customer data will be available and accurate.
- Relevant stakeholders will provide any necessary clarification.
- Project requirements will remain stable throughout the project.

### Questions for the Manager Before Beginning
1. What is the expected deadline for the final report?
2. Which customer data sources should be used?
3. How is customer churn defined for this project?
4. Are there specific business metrics that should be prioritized?
5. Who are the primary stakeholders for the final presentation?
