## Prioritization Method

I used the **Eisenhower Matrix** to prioritize tasks:

- **Urgent and Important:** Report submission, project deadlines, stakeholder meetings.
- **Important but Not Urgent:** Data analysis, report writing, planning.
- **Urgent but Not Important:** Routine emails and administrative tasks.
- **Neither Urgent nor Important:** Activities that do not contribute directly to project goals.

## Buffer Time

I included buffer time each day to accommodate unexpected tasks, urgent requests, and schedule changes without affecting critical project work.
