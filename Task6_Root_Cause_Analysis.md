# Task 6 – Root Cause Analysis and Recovery Plan

## Problem Statement
The customer churn analysis report was delivered two days later than the agreed deadline.

## Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)

**Problem:** The report was submitted late.

**Why 1?**
The analysis took longer than expected.

**Why 2?**
The customer data required additional cleaning and validation before it could be analyzed.

**Why 3?**
The quality issues in the dataset were identified later than planned.

**Why 4?**
A detailed data quality review was not scheduled at the beginning of the project.

**Why 5?**
The project plan underestimated the time required for data preparation and validation.

### Root Cause
Insufficient planning for data preparation resulted in delays during the analysis phase.

---

## Recovery Plan

### Immediate Actions
- Complete the remaining analysis.
- Finalize and review the report for accuracy.
- Share revised timelines with stakeholders.

### Preventive Actions
- Include a dedicated data validation phase in future project plans.
- Build buffer time into project schedules.
- Conduct weekly progress reviews to identify risks early.
- Communicate potential delays as soon as they are identified.

---

## Lessons Learned

This experience highlighted the importance of realistic planning, early risk identification, and proactive communication. Including contingency time and validating data at the start of a project will improve future project delivery.
