# Task 7 – Responsible AI Use

## AI Prompt

Create a professional email requesting a 30-minute introductory meeting with an external partner to discuss a customer churn analysis project. The email should include a clear subject line, a brief introduction, the purpose of the meeting, suggested meeting times, and a professional closing.

---

## AI-Generated Draft

**Subject:** Introductory Meeting Request

Dear Dr. Priya Nathan,

I hope you are doing well.

I am writing to request a 30-minute meeting to discuss our customer churn analysis project and explore potential collaboration between our organizations.

Would you be available next week? Please let me know a time that works for you.

Kind regards,

Njoku Gloria Iheoma

---

## Final Edited Version

**Subject:** Request for a 30-Minute Introductory Meeting – Customer Churn Project

Dear Dr. Priya Nathan,

I hope you are doing well.

My name is Njoku Gloria Iheoma, and I am a Research Associate at Daintymindz. We are currently working on a customer churn analysis project and believe that Meridian Insights' expertise would be valuable to this initiative.

I would like to request a 30-minute introductory meeting next week to discuss potential collaboration, share our project objectives, and explore how our teams can work together.

Would you be available on Tuesday at 10:00 AM or Thursday at 2:00 PM? If neither time is convenient, I would be happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you for considering this invitation. I look forward to the opportunity to connect with you.

Kind regards,

**Njoku Gloria Iheoma**  
Research Associate  
Daintymindz

---

## Reflection

I used AI to generate an initial draft and structure for the email. I then reviewed the content carefully, improved the wording, added specific meeting times, included additional context about the project, and ensured the tone was professional and appropriate. I verified that the final version aligned with the assignment requirements and reflected my own understanding and communication style.

This experience showed me that AI is a useful tool for generating ideas and improving productivity, but human review and editing are essential to ensure accuracy, clarity, and professionalism.
